## H1 Paper 1 (8834/01), 26 Aug 2024, 3h
Format: Sec A Q1 (fieldwork) compulsory; Sec B Q2, Q3 both; Sec C answer 2 of Q4–6 [13 each].

**Q1 Fieldwork – Elderly (Cluster 2 Fieldwork).** Bukit Merah: accessibility of public transport & quality of outdoor spaces for elderly. Hypothesis: 'Public transportation and outdoor spaces in Bukit Merah do not adequately meet the needs of elderly residents.' 2 weekdays Aug 9am–12pm; Bukit Merah Bus Interchange + Tiong Bahru Plaza; survey of 30 elderly (60+) + photos. R1 photos, R2 survey form (Yes/Sometimes/No), R3 survey results table.
- (a) Explain why this hypothesis is appropriate for the investigation. [4]
- (b) Describe how the students can improve the sampling method used. [3]
- (c) With reference to R1 & R2, explain two possible issues related to ethics when collecting primary data. [4]
- (d) With reference to R1, suggest how students could minimise potential risks at Bukit Merah Bus Interchange & Tiong Bahru Plaza. [6]
- (e) With reference to R3, explain the limitations of the data representation shown. [3]
- (f) Evaluate the validity of the data in R3 in answering the hypothesis. [10]
- Ans keys: (a) clearly defined variables/group, scale of sites, timing (non-peak), methods; (b) larger sample, stratified sampling, different times; (c) informed consent, anonymity/photos; (d) crowds, stranger safety (pairs), traffic hazards (bus lanes), disruption (mall permission); (e) table hard to read/compare; (f) small sample (30), 2 sites, narrow time window, quantitative only, subjective wording, no weekends.

**Q2 Climate change (Sec B, Cluster 1 Sustainable Future & CC).** R4 world map of temperature change since mid-19th C; R5 20 largest cumulative CO2 emitters 1850–2021 (fossil vs land use).
- (a) Describe pattern of temperature anomalies in R4. [4] – Arctic >2.5°C, high latitudes most; N hemisphere oceans > S; cooling south of Greenland.
- (b) Compare patterns of CO2 emissions from top ten countries in R5. [4] – developed = fossil fuels (USA 420bt fossil, 85bt land use); Brazil/Indonesia ~90% land use; China fossil; Germany negative land use.
- (c) Explain why areas of greatest temp increase (R4) don't correspond to countries with greatest CO2 emissions (R5). [4] – GHGs mix globally; ice-albedo positive feedback in Arctic.
- (d) Explain two arguments that contemporary CC is caused by human activities. [4] – GHG rise 280→410ppm; isotopic signature; climate models.
- (e) Describe two natural causes of CC leading to glacial and inter-glacial periods. [6] – Milankovitch (eccentricity 100k, obliquity 41k, precession 26k); volcanic activity.

**Q3 Urban reimaging / SUD.** R6 photos London Docklands 1968 & 2018; R7 population of Docklands boroughs (Newham, Southwark, Tower Hamlets) 1801–2019; R8 article on Canary Wharf (Elizabeth Line, Wood Wharf 4,000 homes, Eden Project green spine).
- (a) Using R6, describe changes in London Docklands 1968–2018. [4]
- (b) Explain the economic reasons for urban reimaging efforts. [4] – investment, tourism.
- (c) Describe changes in population of Docklands towns 1801–2019 (R7). [4] – rise to 1901, fall to 1981, rise after.
- (d) With reference to R8, explain impacts of urban reimaging on SUD. [6] – economic/social/environmental sustainability.
- (e) Explain tensions between SUD and liveability. [4] – green premium → unaffordability; density → overcrowding.
