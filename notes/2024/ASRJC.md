**Q1 Fieldwork – Green Hub @ Bishan (community garden + 24h community fridge, void deck Blk 112).** Hypothesis: "Green Hub @ Bishan is effective in encouraging sustainable practices among residents living in its vicinity." 4 students, Fri 3–5pm; 1 counted fridge users (12 in 2h); others surveyed Blk 112 & 113, convenience sampling, 42 residents (25 aged >60). R1 map + photos; R2 recording sheet; R3 survey extract (Q1 visited garden 36Y/6N; Q2 use fridge all the time 3/often 10/sometimes 14/never 15; Q3 encouraged sustainable practices SA3/A21/D10/SD8).
- (a) Suggest one limitation of the hypothesis. [2] – "sustainable practice"/"vicinity" undefined; scale too large.
- (b) Explain how students could minimise risks. [4] – lone student → pairs; small roads → traffic.
- (c) Suggest why sampling was not representative of residents in vicinity. [4] – size, age skew (25/42 >60), only 2 blocks.
- (d) With ref to R2, explain strengths and limitations of the recording sheet. [5] – short/few Qs, captures profile vs ambiguous options ("often"), all closed.
- (e) Explain one limitation of data representation in R3 and sketch a diagram to better represent Q3 data. [5] – pie chart.
- (f) Students concluded Green Hub largely effective. Evaluate validity of conclusion. [10]

**Q2 Weathering, karst, mass movement (Cluster 2).** R6 weathered iron-bearing rock; R7 limestone region, Jamaica (cone & cockpit karst); R8 rainfall intensity–duration threshold for debris flows; R9 schematic slope (permeable soil + cracked bedrock over impervious bedrock).
- (a) With ref to R6, account for the likely weathering process. [4] – oxidation (ferric oxide, red-brown).
- (b) With ref to R7, explain how features of the limestone region developed. [6] – cockpit/cone karst: carbonation, dolines merge, residual cones.
- (c) Explain how climatic factors may influence weathering of rocks. [6] – temperature (Van't Hoff), rainfall, via vegetation.
- (d) With ref to R8, explain how rainfall characteristics could influence occurrence of debris flows. [6] – intensity (Hortonian), duration (pore pressure).
- (e) With ref to R9, account for the likely mass movement. [4] – rotational slide/slump.
- (f) Explain how human factors may influence mass movements on slopes. [4] – loading, road cuts, vibration, deforestation; stabilisation (soil nailing, netting).
