## H2 Paper 1 (9173/01), 22 Aug 2024
**Q1 Resources: solar India, transboundary water (China's Medog 'super dam'), RE share (Cluster 1).** R1 map solar power projects by Indian state 2017 (Telangana >2 GW; south strongest; Jharkhand only eastern state); R2 Wired article "India Is Using More Solar Energy—but It Carries a Lead Risk" (lead-acid batteries, unregulated recycling, villages 2–5 h electricity); R3 China's Himalayan 'super dam' Medog on upper Brahmaputra (Yarlung Tsangpo) + existing dams; R4 SDG 7.2.1 share of final energy from renewables 2020.
- (a) Explain how provisioning services can be classified based on their renewability. [4] – stock vs renewable; critical/non-critical zones; continuum.
- (b) With ref to R1, describe distribution of solar power projects in India in 2017. [4]
- (c) With ref to R2, explain the trade-offs associated with the use of solar power. [4] – emissions cut vs lead pollution; village electricity vs health.
- (d) With ref to R3, explain how China's super dam in Tibet can cause conflict between China and India. [6] – quantity, quality, timing, barrier (fish, sediment).
- (e) Explain strategies to mitigate potential conflict over transboundary water resources (R3). [6] – international water agreements, river basin commissions, PES.
- (f) With ref to R4, suggest possible reasons for global variation in share of energy from renewables. [6] – physical, economic (Chinese investment in Africa), political.

**Q2 Monsoon climate, tropical cyclone, deltas – Myanmar (Cluster 2).** R5 climograph Pathein (17°N; 26.2°C, 2330 mm, Am, wet May–Oct); R6 summer & winter monsoon circulation, humid tropical Asia; R7 forecast track Tropical Cyclone Mocha May 2023 (Bay of Bengal → Cat-4 landfall Myanmar 15 May); R8 Irrawaddy (Ayeyarwady) Delta land cover 1974 vs 2021 (mangrove loss, irrigated crops, built-up).
- (a) Describe climatic characteristics of Pathein (R5). [4]
- (b) Using R6, account for the climatic characteristics of Pathein (R5). [6]
- (c) With ref to R7, describe the development of Tropical Cyclone Mocha. [4]
- (d) Explain atmospheric and surface conditions necessary for development of tropical cyclones such as in R7. [5] – SST >26°C to 60 m, Coriolis >5°, low shear, upper divergence.
- (e) With ref to R8, explain how ecosystem services of the Irrawaddy Delta could be affected by changes in land cover. [6]
- (f) Explain how deltas are formed. [5] – river/wave/tide-dominated.
