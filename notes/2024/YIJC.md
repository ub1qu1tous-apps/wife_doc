**Q1 Fieldwork – Transport needs of the elderly, Serangoon North.** 10 × 18-yr-olds; elderly = 30% of local pop (60% aged 65–74, 35% 75–84, 5% 84+; 55% female). Hypothesis: "The majority of elderly residents in the Serangoon North neighbourhood find their transport needs adequately met". Survey Friday 10am–12noon at a bus stop; 80 elderly (55/20/5 by age band). R1 photo of the location (bus stop by road bend); R2 questionnaire (age bands, gender, resident?, years; satisfaction 1–5 for public transport, frequency, safety, footpaths, ride/car-sharing, emergency transport; 2 biggest advantages); R3 results of those "most satisfied" by age group (incl. "no data").
- (a) Explain whether the students' hypothesis is suitable. [4] – "adequately met" vs survey's "most satisfied".
- (b) Suggest how students could minimise two potential risks at the location in R1. [4] – weather, traffic/PMAs; don't just change location.
- (c) Suggest two reasons why students categorised the age groups as in R2 & R3. [4] – match estate profile; young-old/mid-old/old-old needs differ.
- (d) Suggest two possible issues related to research ethics for this investigation. [4]
- (e) Explain the best way to present the data from the questionnaire in R3. [4] – bar graph/radar; not pie.
- (f) Students concluded transport needs of the majority of elderly are adequately met. Evaluate validity. [10]
