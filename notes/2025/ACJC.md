**Q2 El Niño, tropical cyclones, hydrographs, meandering vs braided, deltas (Cluster 2).** R5 global SST anomaly map, El Niño 2023 (ref 1991–2020; +1.5–3°C central/eastern equatorial Pacific); R6 world map of tropical cyclone areas, % of global total and seasons (E Asia 33% Jul–Nov; E Pacific 17% Jun–Oct; Caribbean 11% Aug–Nov; Bay of Bengal 6% May–Nov; Arabian Sea 2% May–Nov; W Australia 13%, Madagascar 11%, SW Pacific 7% Nov–Apr; NH 69%, SH 31%); R7 aerial photo of a meandering rainforest river with cross-section P (outer bank) – Q (point bar); R8 braided stream on glacial outwash plain (NZ-type, snow-capped mountains) with cross-section X–Y.

[6] – SST >26–27°C, 5–25°N/S; late summer/autumn each hemisphere (NH Jun–Nov, SH Nov–Apr); Coriolis absent <5°; easterly waves (Atlantic ~60%/85% intense); humidity, low wind shear; westward tracks by trades. Examiner: address NH vs SH timing.
- (c) Explain how climate and vegetation lead to differences in storm hydrographs of the humid and arid tropics.
