## H1 Paper 1 (8834/01), 17 Sep 2025
**Q1 Fieldwork – Liveability for the elderly, Toa Payoh, Singapore.** Four 17-year-olds; hypothesis: 'The elderly aged 65 years and above living in Toa Payoh experience a high level of liveability.' Age-Friendly Cities factors: community support & health services; outdoor spaces & buildings; transportation. Survey in May in a central area of Toa Payoh on two weekday afternoons; target 50 not reached → asked peers with elderly relatives in Toa Payoh to administer it; plus face-to-face interviews with 6 elderly; on-site surveys recording physical features. R1 photos of survey sites (an exit of Toa Payoh MRT; outside a hawker centre – which appears closed for cleaning); R2 data collection sheet – **Likert 1 = Strongly Agree … 5 = Strongly Disagree** (reversed), items: clinic <500 m; elder care centre <500 m; barrier-free; wayfinding; sufficient open spaces; sufficient exercise corners; bus stop <500 m; cycling paths connected & safe; R3 mean scores – clinic 3.68, eldercare 3.66 (factor 3.67); barrier-free 3.26, find way 3.82, open spaces 2.92, exercise corners 2.22 (factor 3.05); bus stop 4.08, cycling 3.18 (factor 3.63); R4 photos – worn desire path down a slope near Blk 211; Toa Payoh East dementia-friendly wayfinding motifs (blue sunflowers, red palm trees) painted on HDB pillars.

- (b) With reference to Resource 1, suggest the challenges that the students may face when conducting the survey. [4] – language barrier (English-only sheet); unwilling/rude passers-by; few passers-by on weekday afternoons; hawker centre closed.

- (d) Explain the ethical considerations that the students should take note of when conducting interviews with the elderly. [4] – appointments, informed (written) consent, confidentiality/pseudonyms, dignity/no harm, power imbalance, translation (dialects, Malay).
- (e) Use a sketch graph to represent the 'mean score for each factor' shown in Resource 3. [4] – bar graph; title, axis labels, accurate bars.
