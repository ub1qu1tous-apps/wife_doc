**Q1 Fieldwork – Silver Zones & needs of the elderly, Ang Mo Kio St 61 & Hougang St 61.** Silver Zones = residential areas with high proportion of elderly; >40 by end-2024. 6 students; hypothesis "Silver Zones in Singapore have met the needs of elderly above the age of 65 years old." Internet research on generic Silver Zone features → chose 2 streets → site recce 2 days before. Questionnaire to 100 residents per street, 9am–6pm, 4 sites per street, 2 days in Dec holidays; picked "elderly-looking people walking by", verified age, sought consent. R1 map of the two streets and 8 sites; R2 photos of two sites (AMK central, Hougang NE; photos from pre-fieldwork internet search & recce); R3 questionnaire (age 65–69…85+; site; 5-pt Likert, with photos: Q1 additional green man time helpful; Q2 speed limit makes me feel safer; Q3 ramp-down & two-stage crossings → walk with more confidence; Q4 anti-slip coating on drain covers); R4 results % (SA/A/N/D/SD) – AMK: Q1 33/27/30/10/0, Q2 64/16/10/10/0, Q3 10/5/67/8/10, Q4 0/10/33/27/30; Hougang: Q1 60/7/13/10/10, Q2 10/10/70/10/0, Q3 1/2/88/9/0, Q4 5/36/5/30/24.
- (a) Explain two weaknesses of the students' hypothesis. [4] – scale (all SG vs 2 streets, 6 students, 2 days); "needs of the elderly" undefined; not measurable by questionnaire alone.

[6] – strengths: spread along whole street/different blocks (Blk 621 vs 639), shelter, wide sidewalks, public spaces (ethical); weaknesses: 2 of 40 zones, no N/W/S, bias toward elderly near Silver Zone roads, immobile elderly missed.

- (e) The students concluded that Silver Zones in Singapore have met the needs of elderly above the age of 65 years old. With reference to all resources, evaluate the validity of their conclusion.
