- (b) With reference to Resources 5 and 6, explain the role of natural factors in contributing to the occurrence of mass movements. - (c) With reference to Resources 6 and 7, compare the characteristics of the mass movements shown in Bujumbura and the Valley of the Kings. [4] – similar: weathering weakens rock, shear stress > strength, rapid; different: flows/slides vs falls/block slides; soft regolith vs angular blocks/talus.

- (e) Explain the dominance of physical weathering processes over chemical weathering processes in arid environments. [6] – little moisture inhibits carbonation/hydrolysis; high diurnal range → thermal expansion; evaporation → salt crystal growth.
- (f) With reference to Resource 8 explain how rock weathering affects slope stability. [6] – ↓ intact strength; more/persistent/wider discontinuities = planes of weakness, ↑ permeability → feedback; clays/regolith in humid tropics; ↓ shear strength.
