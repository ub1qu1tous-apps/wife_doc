## H1 Geography 8834
Paper: Sec A Q1 = Cluster 2 Fieldwork (30m); Sec B Q2, Q3 = Cluster 1 structured (22m each, "may be on a specific topic or a combination of topics"); Sec C Q4–Q6 = Cluster 1 essays (13m, answer 2).

**Cluster 1: Sustainable Future and Climate Change**
- **1.1 Cities in a Sustainable Future**
  - 1.1.1 Sustainable Urban Development — what is SD; measuring SUD; urban population trends (rapid urban growth, urban population loss); demand on natural environments (waste, ecological footprint)
  - 1.1.2 Sustainable Cities — waste management (non-hazardous solid waste); slum management; urban reimaging
  - 1.1.3 Liveable Cities — SUD vs liveability (subjective nature, factors, complementarity/tension); liveable cities for the elderly; liveable cities for women
- **1.2 The Future with Climate Change**
  - 1.2.1 The Science of Climate Change — past climate variability (proxies, Quaternary); natural factors (solar output, thermohaline circulation, ice sheets, feedbacks); human influence (IPCC consensus, carbon cycle, enhanced greenhouse effect, positive/negative feedbacks)
  - 1.2.2 Possible Effects of Climate Change — changes in temp & precipitation; impacts on aquatic/terrestrial ecosystems & humans; impacts on places (+/−), variations (uneven changes, vulnerability); uncertainty over impacts
  - 1.2.3 Responses to Climate Change — mitigation; adaptation; key actors (firms, NGOs, state) & partnerships; varying success & challenges (economic, political, uncertainty)

**Cluster 2: Fieldwork** — areas: (a) Community response to climate change; (b) Needs analysis of the elderly living in an urban neighbourhood.

## H2 Geography 9173
Paper 1: Q1 = Cluster 1 structured; Q2 = Cluster 2 structured; Sec B Q3/Q4 = Cluster 1 essay, Q5/Q6 = Cluster 2 essay (20m).
Paper 2: Q1 = Cluster 4 Fieldwork; Q2 = Cluster 3 structured; Sec C Q3/Q4 = Cluster 3 essay (20m).

**Cluster 1: Development, Economy and Environment**
- **1.1 Environment and Resources**
  - 1.1.1 Understanding Sustainable Development — concept of SD, trade-offs; SDGs & indicators; challenges limiting progress (economic, political)
  - 1.1.2 Environment and Resources — ecosystem services; human activities compromising them; renewable/non-renewable; reserves classification; Malthus & Boserup
  - 1.1.3 Managing Resources — renewable energy (solar, hydropower: potential, limits, trade-offs); transboundary water conflicts; extractive industries (characteristics, impacts, strategies)
- **1.2 Development and the Global Economy**
  - 1.2.1 Development — variations in levels of development; global flows of trade, capital, labour; shifts in manufacturing/services/agriculture & economic structure
  - 1.2.2 Geography of the Global Economy and TNCs — interconnectedness via flows; GPNs of TNCs; impacts of GPNs on home/host economies
  - 1.2.3 Relative Influence of Actors in Shaping the Global Economy — states; labour (labour characteristics, unions); multilateral institutions (ASEAN, WTO, World Bank)

**Cluster 2: Tropical Environments**
- **2.1 Tropical Climates and Drainage Basins**
  - 2.1.1 Tropical Climates — climate zones (Af, Am, Aw, BSh, BWh); Hadley cells & ITCZ; monsoons; tropical cyclones; ENSO
  - 2.1.2 Drainage Basin Hydrology — inputs/outputs (water balance); stores; pathways; natural & human factors
  - 2.1.3 Floods in the Humid Tropics — factors (natural/human); impacts & varying vulnerability; flood management strategies
- **2.2 Landforms in the Tropics**
  - 2.2.1 Geomorphic Processes — weathering (chemical/physical, factors); mass movement & water erosion on slopes; fluvial processes
  - 2.2.2 Karst Landforms in the Humid Tropics — cockpit/tower karst, caves; formation factors; ecosystem services & human impact
  - 2.2.3 Fluvial Landforms in the Humid Tropics — meanders, braided channels, deltas; formation factors; delta ecosystem services & human impact

**Cluster 3: Sustainable Future and Climate Change**
- **3.1 Cities in a Sustainable Future**
  - 3.1.1 Sustainable Urban Development — measuring SUD (dimensions, indicators, difficulties); urban population trends; demand on natural environments (waste, ecological footprint)
  - 3.1.2 Sustainable Cities — waste management; slum management; urban reimaging
  - 3.1.3 Liveable Cities — SUD vs liveability; elderly; women
- **3.2 The Future with Climate Change**
  - 3.2.1 The Science of Climate Change
  - 3.2.2 Possible Effects of Climate Change
  - 3.2.3 Responses to Climate Change
  (content as H1 1.2.1–1.2.3)

**Cluster 4: Fieldwork** — areas: (a) Community response to climate change; (b) Needs analysis of the elderly living in an urban neighbourhood; (c) Fluvial flood risk and strategies to mitigate it.

## Labelling convention
- Cell/label = code + short name, e.g. `1.2.2 Effects of CC`; a structured question spanning sub-topics lists each, e.g. `1.2.1 Science of CC; 1.2.3 Responses to CC`.
- Fieldwork = `Fieldwork – <area>`, e.g. `Fieldwork – Elderly needs`, `Fieldwork – CC response`, `Fieldwork – Flood risk`; if the scenario is outside the 2/3 syllabus areas, say so, e.g. `Fieldwork – Other (transport)`.
