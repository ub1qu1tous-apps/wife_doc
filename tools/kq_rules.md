# Key-question (KQ) labelling rules

Task: for every paper section in a notes file (`## H1 Paper 1 ...`, `## H2 Paper 1 ...`, `## H2 Paper 2 ...`), add ONE new line directly
after the existing `Syllabus topics:` line:

    Syllabus KQs: Q1 <codes> | Q2 <codes> | Q3 <codes> | ...

- One entry per question slot, same slots/order as the `Syllabus topics:` line (H1: Q1–Q6; H2 P1: Q1–Q6; H2 P2: Q1–Q4).
- `<codes>` = KQ codes separated by `; ` (codes only, no words), e.g. `Q2 1.2.1c; 1.2.1b; 1.2.2a`.
- Fieldwork questions (H1 Q1, H2 P2 Q1): copy the fieldwork label exactly as on the Syllabus topics line, e.g. `Q1 Fieldwork – Elderly needs`.
- Structured questions: look at EVERY sub-part (a)–(h); give the KQ each sub-part tests; list each KQ once, ordered by total marks (highest first). Usually 2–5 codes.
- Essays: the KQ the essay question is about (normally 1 code; 2 only if the question clearly spans two).
- Only use codes from the lists below. Codes must stay inside the sub-topic(s) already on the Syllabus topics line unless the question wording clearly shows otherwise.
- Don't change any other line of the file. Don't commit.

Worked example (ACJC 2024, already agreed with the user):
- H1: `Syllabus KQs: Q1 Fieldwork – Elderly needs | Q2 1.2.1c; 1.2.1b; 1.2.2a; 1.2.2b | Q3 1.1.2c; 1.1.1b; 1.1.3a | Q4 1.1.1b | Q5 1.2.2b | Q6 1.2.3c`

   Q4 'Rapid urban growth is the greatest challenge for SUD' → 1.1.1b urban population trends.)

Guidance on the trickier boundaries:
- Temperature/precipitation changes, impacts on ecosystems and on humans → x.2.2a. 'Affects all regions equally / everyone the same way', vulnerability, LDCs vs DCs → x.2.2b. Uncertainty → x.2.2c.
- Mitigation vs adaptation, specific strategies → x.2.3a. Firms/NGOs/states/partnerships → x.2.3b. 'Effective/successful?', challenges, 'all places able to implement' → x.2.3c.
- Glacial/interglacial, proxies, Quaternary, ice/ocean cores → x.2.1a. Solar output, thermohaline, ice sheets, natural feedbacks, 'natural factors account for' → x.2.1b. Carbon cycle, GHG, IPCC, human-driven feedbacks, emissions by country → x.2.1c.
- Measuring SUD/indicators (H2 3.1.1a) vs H1 1.1.1a 'what is SD' (concept, Brundtland, dimensions). Urban growth, urbanisation rates, shrinking cities/urban population loss → x.1.1b. Ecological footprint, waste generation, resource consumption, demand on environment → x.1.1c.
- Waste strategies/problems → x.1.2a; slums → x.1.2b; reimaging → x.1.2c. Liveability (indices, factors, subjective, tension with SUD) → x.1.3a; elderly → x.1.3b; women/gender → x.1.3c.
- H2 Cluster 1: SD concept/trade-offs 1.1.1a; SDGs/indicators/measuring 1.1.1b; challenges to SD 1.1.1c; ecosystem services 1.1.2a; reserves (proven/conditional/hypothetical), renewability, depletion 1.1.2b; Malthus/Boserup 1.1.2c; solar/hydro/renewables 1.1.3a; transboundary rivers 1.1.3b; extractive industries 1.1.3c.
  Levels of development across space 1.2.1a; trade/FDI/capital/labour flows 1.2.1b; economic structure, sector shifts, employment structure 1.2.1c; interdependence via flows 1.2.2a; GPN connections (sourcing, production, distribution, supply chains, TNC locations) 1.2.2b; GPN impacts on home/host 1.2.2c; states 1.2.3a; labour/unions 1.2.3b; ASEAN/WTO/World Bank/MIs 1.2.3c.
- H2 Cluster 2: climate zones, describing climographs, temperature characteristics 2.1.1a; Hadley cell/ITCZ/global circulation 2.1.1b; monsoon, cyclones, ENSO 2.1.1c; water balance 2.1.2a; storage 2.1.2b; pathways, hydrographs, infiltration/overland flow 2.1.2c; flood causes 2.1.3a; flood impacts/vulnerability 2.1.3b; flood management/control 2.1.3c; weathering 2.2.1a; mass movement, slope processes, landslides 2.2.1b; fluvial erosion/transport/deposition 2.2.1c; karst landforms/features/caves 2.2.2a; karst formation/factors 2.2.2b; karst ecosystem services/human impact 2.2.2c; describing meanders/braided/deltas 2.2.3a; their formation/factors (incl. waves/tides for deltas) 2.2.3b; delta ecosystem services/human impact 2.2.3c.
